## Reviewed setup facts

- Four Level I technologies: Terraforming, Advanced Genetics, Narrow Beam Lasers, Advanced Robotics; shuffle into four slots. Technology deck counts II/III/IIII: 16/16/10. The 46 identities are inventoried; later effects require their own source-reviewed implementation before actions become available.
- Six face-down Events per II/III level: its two fixed guild Events plus four randomly selected from eight others, shuffled. Fixed II: Maya Chaturvedi and Tyler Lee. Fixed III: KC-24X and Onu. Unselected Events are out of the game.
- Nineteen technology slots: 4 I, 6 II, 6 III, 3 IIII. The printed direct edge from I-3 to III-5 is intentional. Top/middle IIII slots require 2 of 3 prerequisites; bottom requires both. This is not the invented graph in the concept illustration.
- Basic Spacefaring has 2/3/4 Level I research spaces and 2/3/4 Level II spaces. The last 1/1/2 Level II spaces respectively cost an extra Ore. Build/jump has two spaces; the second costs an extra Ore only at two players. The last action is repeatable. Action pawns begin off-board.
- All four Guilds are covered. Terraforming and Professors’ are Tier 1; Technocrats’ and Meta-Science are Tier 2. Cover counts 1/2/1/2 action spaces.
- Standard Exploration board: two A, two B, three controllable shipyards, Sol, Deep Space. Two-player face: one A, two B, one controllable shipyard, Sol, Deep Space. Sol and Deep Space have no control disc. Both exact route graphs are in the manifest.
- Starting A identities: Barnard’s Star, Lalande 21185, Sirius, Wolf 359. Reveal 1 at two players, otherwise 2; shuffle other Starting cards into remaining A. Shuffle B and reveal 2. Both System decks contain 12 cards.
- Always Transcendence + Empire; Introductory adds Technologist (A) + Warlord (B). Randomized selects one of four A and one of four B. Disable the second A reward with 2–3 players. Endgame threshold 3/3/4 total claims.
- All Basic mats: Supply capacities A–E = 5/4/3/3/2; two Population; one level-1 ship at Sol; eight Food and eight Ore discs covering their tracks; automation zero. Food exposure sequence A/blank/B/blank/C/blank/D/blank/E; each Ore position yields one Ore. Ore by order 1/1/2/2 before faction bonuses. Resource cubes are never rolled during setup.
- Nishida-Østergaard: +2 Ore. Disciples: +1 Ore, starting ship in Deep Space; excluded at two players under the plan's publisher setup sheet. Zheng-Yi: convert one Supply from A into an additional level-1 ship at Sol. United Nations: convert one Supply from C into an additional Population. The special columns matter; ordinary leftmost Supply conversion is not used for these exceptions.

## Milestone004 — first ordinary research turn

Authority remains the second-print publisher PDF: printed pp. 7–10 (pawn relocation, exclusive production branch, achievement phase), pp. 13–18 (cube conversion, research, immediate bonuses, automation and upgrades), and p. 22 (mandatory effects and exceptions). The four printed Level I component faces listed above supply their exact immediate bonuses. The concept plates supply composition only.

- A Basic Level I space is free of Ore cost, requires one available Population for permanent placement, and must be open. The same pawn must leave its current space. A different faction’s permanent research cube never occupies an Action space. Every faction may research the same discovered card once; there are no Level I prerequisites.
- Terraforming converts leftmost Supply to a level-1 ship in Deep Space. Advanced Genetics automates the leftmost Food disc, then converts leftmost Supply to available Population. Advanced Robotics automates the leftmost Ore disc and gains one Ore. Narrow Beam Lasers gains one Ore and upgrades one own ship by one level, capped at four; the ship choice is staged before payment. Action boxes printed on these cards are future actions and do not execute as bonuses.
- Production chooses exactly one of growth, Ore or trade. Growth draws one cube from each exposed letter’s matching column, skipping empty columns. Each exposed Ore circle gives one Ore. Trade applies the three printed rates, may repeat, and finishes without either ordinary production yield. Returns fill rightmost empty Supply spaces, overflowing into A only when all printed spaces are full.
- The turn projection conserves all twenty cubes across Supply, available Population, ships and permanent research. The setup seed, factions, topology, settings and concealed decks remain unchanged. Root/event transactions authorize the active remote identity or the shared-table controller operating that faction. The append-only history restores a pending production/trade phase instead of repeating a bonus.
- Level-I-only play cannot meet a base achievement: it cannot research II/III/IIII, colonize, gain system control, create four ships, reach the end of a production track, or empty Supply to two cubes. The empty achievement phase therefore resolves automatically into the real clockwise handoff, with no redundant acknowledgement. Its existing end-turn event preserves replay and recovery. Claim machinery is introduced with the mechanics and milestone that can satisfy a requirement; no fictitious claim or score is recorded.

Milestones007/008 will deepen production/exchange journeys at changed tracks, exhaustion and fleet-control consequences. Their basic production and exchange mechanics are intentionally shared with this first complete turn. Discovery, ship movement, colonization and card action spaces remain outside004; their printed text remains inspectable and is not an implemented-action claim.
